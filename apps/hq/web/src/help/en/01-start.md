# Getting started

HQ is MyWineCorks' own back office. Here we manage website requests, clients, subscriptions and license keys, cloud workspaces, AI usage, software releases and HQ staff. Clients never see HQ.

## Signing in for the first time

1. A Super Admin adds you under **HQ staff** and sends you a **setup link** (valid 7 days, works once).
2. Open the link and choose a password of **at least 12 characters**.
3. Set up **2-step verification**: scan the code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password…) and enter the 6-digit code. Every HQ account must have it.
4. From then on, sign in with your email, password and the 6-digit code.

> After 5 wrong tries the sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes. If your link has expired or you lost your authenticator, ask a Super Admin for a **New setup link**.

## Language

Switch between 中文 and EN at the top right. Emails to a client use the client's own **Email language**, not yours.

## Roles

What you can do depends on your role. The server checks this on every action, not only the screen.

- **Super Admin** — everything, including revoking licenses, releases, HQ staff and the audit log.
- **Sales** — clients and website requests, subscriptions, issuing and renewing license keys.
- **Support** — clients and website requests, moving a license to a new server.
- **Finance** — subscriptions and payments.
- **Read-only** — can look at everything except HQ staff and the audit log, and change nothing.

## The pages

- **Dashboard** — renewals due, alerts from servers, and the latest release.
- **Website requests** — demo, trial and contact forms from MyWineCorks.com.
- **Clients** — every client with subscriptions, licenses, payments and emails.
- **Installations** — every server that uses a license.
- **Cloud workspaces** — clients who use our cloud instead of their own server.
- **AI usage** — how much of the AI assistant each license uses.
- **Releases** — update packages for self-hosted servers and the downloads on the website.
- **HQ staff** and **Audit log** — Super Admin only.
