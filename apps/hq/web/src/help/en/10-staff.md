# HQ staff and audit log

Both pages are for Super Admins only.

## HQ staff

- **Add staff** — email, name and **Role**. HQ shows a **Setup link** (valid 7 days); send it to the person. They set their password and 2-step verification.
- **New setup link** — for someone whose link expired or who lost their authenticator app. Their old password stops working.
- **Deactivate** / **Reactivate** — someone who leaves is deactivated, never deleted, so their history stays.
- You cannot change your own role or deactivate yourself, and HQ always keeps at least one active Super Admin.

## Audit log

Every change in HQ is recorded: who, when, what changed (before and after) and the reason — sign-ins, clients, subscriptions, keys issued, renewed, moved or revoked, payments, releases, staff changes, website requests and client downloads. The log cannot be edited or deleted.
