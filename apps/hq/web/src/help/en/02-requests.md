# Website requests

Every form sent on MyWineCorks.com (book a demo, start a trial, contact us) lands under **Website requests**, with what the visitor told us: name, company, email, phone, country, how many bottles, whether they sell wine or keep their own collection, and whether they want our cloud or their own server.

## Working a request

1. Open the newest **New** requests first. Filter by status at the top.
2. Contact the person, then press **Mark contacted**.
3. When they become a client, press **Create client**. HQ creates the client with the details from the form (status *Lead* or *Trial*) and marks the request **Client created**. You then add a subscription on the client's page.
4. If nothing comes of it, press **Close**.

> A request can only be turned into a client once. Spam is filtered with a hidden field and a per-address limit; if something slips through, just close it.
