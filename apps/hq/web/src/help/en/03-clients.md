# Clients

**Clients** lists every client with its number (MWC-0001…), status, country, licence expiry and the last time its server checked in. Search by name, number, contact or email.

## Adding a client

Press **New client** and fill in the company or person, contact, email, phone, **Country** (Taiwan or United States), **Email language** and notes. The email is important: license keys, renewal reminders and the sign-in link for the client account on the website all go there.

## Client status

- **Lead** — talking, nothing issued yet. Becomes *Active* when the first license key is issued.
- **Trial** — trying the software.
- **Active** — paying and licensed.
- **Past due** — payment late; set by hand.
- **Locked** — their license has locked.
- **Cancelled** — no longer a client; no renewal reminders are sent.

A renewal sets *Trial*, *Past due* or *Locked* back to *Active*.

## The client page

One page holds everything about a client: details, **Subscriptions**, **Licenses** (with the server each one is used on), **Payments**, **Emails** we sent and the **History** of changes. Press **Edit** to change the details.

## The client account on the website

Clients sign in at **MyWineCorks.com › Client account** with the email we have on file: they get a one-time sign-in link by email. There they see their licenses and expiry dates and, for self-hosted plans, download the installer and guides. If a client cannot sign in, check the email address on their client page.
