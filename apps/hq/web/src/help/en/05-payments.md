# Payments and emails

## Recording a payment

On the client page press **Record payment** and enter the date paid, amount, method (bank transfer, cheque, card, cash, other), a reference and the invoice number. Payments are for our records; recording one does not change the license — renew the license separately.

## Emails

The **Emails** list on the client page shows every email HQ sent to the client: license keys, renewal reminders and sign-in links, with the date and whether it was **sent**. *Not sent (no mail server)* means HQ has no mail server set up yet; send the key another way.
