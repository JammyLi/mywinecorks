# Requesting wines

You order by sending the shop a **request**.

1. On a wine, choose the number of bottles and press **Add**. Some wines have a limit per customer ("Up to 6 bottles").
2. Open **Request** to see everything you added. Change quantities or **Remove** wines.
3. Choose **Delivery to an address** or **Pick-up**. For delivery, pick one of your addresses or enter a **New address**.
4. Add a **Note for the shop** if you like, and press **Send request**.

> A request is not a purchase. Nothing is reserved or charged until the shop confirms it. The total shown is an estimate before tax and shipping; the shop confirms the final prices.

## My requests

**My requests** shows each request and where it stands:

- **Sent** — on its way to the shop.
- **Received** — the shop has it.
- **Confirmed** — the shop accepted it and will contact you about payment and delivery.
- **Declined** — the shop could not accept it.
- **Shipped** — on its way to you.

If a wine is no longer available in the quantity you asked for when you send, you are asked to check your request first.
