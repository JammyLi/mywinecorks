# Browsing the wines

**Wines** shows everything the shop offers you.

- **Search** by wine, producer, region or grape, and filter by **Type** (red, white, sparkling…).
- **Availability** shows **Available**, **Limited** or the number of bottles, depending on the shop. **Sold out** wines can be shown so you can ask to be told when they are back.
- **Prices** are the shop's prices for you. Some wines show **Price on request** — add them to your request and the shop will quote.

## A wine's page

Tap a wine to see its details: country, region, classification, grapes, bottle size, drinking window, scores and the shop's tasting notes.

## Favorites

Tap **♡ Favorite** to keep a wine in **Favorites**, your own short list.

## Tell me when available

On a sold-out wine, choose **Tell me when available**. You get an email when the shop has it again.

## Asking in your own words

If the shop offers it, you can **Ask** in plain words, for example "a red for a steak dinner under 3000", or ask a question on a wine's page, such as which food goes with it. These are AI suggestions — please check with the shop.
