# Getting started

This is your wine shop's private wine list. Only customers the shop has invited can see it, and only the wines and prices the shop has chosen for you.

## Your invitation

1. The shop sends you an invitation link, by email or another way (for example LINE). It works once and is valid for 14 days.
2. Open it and choose a password of **at least 10 characters**.
3. Confirm that you are of legal drinking age.

That's all — you are signed in and see the wine list.

## Signing in

Open the address the shop gave you and sign in with your email and password. You stay signed in on that device for 30 days.

> **Forgot your password?** Ask the shop to send you a new invitation; with it you choose a new password. After 5 wrong tries, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.

## No invitation?

On the sign-in page, choose **Ask for access** and leave your name, email, phone and a message. The shop decides who can see its wines and sends you an invitation if it approves.

## Language

Switch between 中文 and English at the top. Your choice is kept in **My details**.
