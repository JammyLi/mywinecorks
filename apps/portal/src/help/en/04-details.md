# Your details and privacy

## My details

In **My details** you can update your name, phone, language and **Addresses**. Changes are sent to the shop, which keeps your customer record.

## Privacy

- You only see the wines and prices the shop has chosen for you. Other customers never see your details, prices or requests.
- Your password is stored securely; the shop cannot see it.

## When access ends

If the shop ends your access, you see "Your access to this wine list has ended". Contact the shop if you think this is a mistake.

## Need help?

For anything about wines, prices, payment or delivery, contact the shop directly — they run this wine list.
