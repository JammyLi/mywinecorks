## How storage is organised

**Cellar › Locker › Rack.** A cellar is a place (your office cellar, a warehouse, a storage company). A locker is a cabinet, room or unit in it; a rack is a shelf or slot. Bottles are either **loose** on a rack or in a **case** (original wooden case or carton) that sits on a rack.

Create cellars in **Storage** (Owner), then lockers and racks. Each locker has a hidden "unknown rack" for bottles whose exact place is not known yet; they show as *not sure* until someone scans them in place.

## Numbers and labels

Every wine, bottle and case has its own number, and every label has a QR code:

- **W-000123** — a wine (producer, name, vintage, size)
- **B-0004567** — one bottle
- a case number such as **11-26-26-03** (month-day-year and a counter) — one case

Numbers are never reused, so a printed label always points to the same thing. (Coming from MyWines? Your existing labels keep working.)

## Printing

Open **Labels**, choose the kind (bottle, case, locker or rack) and which ones — bottles without a printed label yet, a list, search results, numbers you type, or all cases in a locker. Print on a **label printer** or on **label sheets in an office printer** (A4 / Letter; set the label size). Check the actual-size preview, and print from the browser at 100 % scale — do not let the printer "fit to page".

Tip: print rack labels once and stick them on the shelves; scanning the rack label is the fastest way to say "here".
