## Search

**Search** shows bottles grouped by wine. Type part of a producer, name or region, and narrow down with the filters: type, country, region, vintage range, price range, cellar, locker, rack, merchant, status. Each choice shows how many bottles it has. Switch between **By wine** and **By case** to see individual cases and where they are; open a wine to see each of its bottles.

Columns show quantity, location, market price and value — and, for roles allowed to see them, cost and gain.

## Searching in your own words (AI)

Type what you are looking for in the box above the filters — *“2010 Bordeaux under 3,000 in Neihu”*, *“香檳 5000 以下”* — and press **Search in your words**. MyWineCorks turns it into the normal filters, which you can see and change.

## Saved lists

Select bottles or wines and **Save as list** for a tasting, an insurance schedule or a customer offer. A list can be turned into an order, used as a portal catalog, or printed as labels.

## Export

Every search can be exported to Excel with the columns you choose. Cost and profit columns are only offered to roles that may see them.
