## Before you start

The portal needs the *Portal* module in your plan. In **Settings › Customer portal** confirm that you may sell alcohol where you and your customers are, choose the portal's address and the age customers must confirm (18+ in Taiwan, 21+ in the US), and switch it on.

## Catalogs

A **catalog** is what a group of customers may see: chosen wines, a saved list, or a rule (for example "all Champagne in stock"). For each catalog choose:

- **Price** — fixed per wine, from your suggested price, from the market price (± %), or "on request"; the customer's own discount can apply.
- **Stock shown** — exact number, "available / limited", or nothing; an optional limit per customer; whether sold-out wines stay visible.
- **Who** — customer groups (VIP, restaurants…) or single customers; start and end dates.

When a customer is in several catalogs, the one with the higher priority sets the price.

## Inviting a customer

On the customer's page, **Invite** gives you a link to send them (by LINE or email; it works once, for 14 days). They set a password and confirm their age. You can end someone's access at once with **Revoke access**.

## Requests

Customers browse, ask about a wine, and send a **request** with quantities and delivery. It arrives as a **draft order** for you to check, price and confirm. They see its status change. Payment stays between you and the customer.

The portal never shows your cost, locations, bottle or case numbers, or anything about other customers.

## If a customer sees no wines

Check, in this order:

1. **Who sees it** — the catalog must include the customer, or a group they belong to. A catalog with no one in it is shown to no one.
2. **Active and dates** — the catalog is **Active** and today is between its start and end dates.
3. **Wines** — the catalog has wines in stock (sold-out wines only show if **Show sold-out wines as "sold out"** is on).
4. **Sync** — changes reach the portal within a few minutes; **Sync now** in **Settings › Customer portal** sends them at once.

A wine with no price under the catalog's rule (for example no suggested price, with *Suggested price* chosen) still shows, as **Price on request**.

## Help for your customers

Customers have their own short guide in the portal (**Help**, also on the sign-in page): signing in, browsing, favorites, sending a request and their details. You can send them the link `…/<portal address>/help`.
