## Customers

**Customers** keeps contact details, addresses, language, a usual discount and the customer's history: what they bought, at what price, and open balances.

## An order, step by step

1. **New order** — from Search (selected wines), a saved list, a customer's page, a portal request, or **Order from message** (paste a LINE / WhatsApp / email message or a photo; AI matches the wines and quantities for you to check).
2. **Prices** fill in from this customer's last price, their catalog, or your suggested price. The ✦ button suggests a price (AI) based on the market price and this customer's history.
3. **Confirm** — the exact bottles are held for this order. Your role may have a limit on order value or discounts.
4. **Pick** — on the phone, scan each bottle. A wrong bottle is refused.
5. **Ship** — mark shipped (or picked up). The bottles leave stock.
6. **Payments** — record what was paid and how; the order and the customer show what is still open.

Print the **customer copy** or save it as PDF to send (in the customer's language). It never shows cost, locations or bottle numbers.

## Changing or cancelling

Draft and confirmed orders can be changed; cancelling a confirmed order releases its bottles. Every change is in the change log.
