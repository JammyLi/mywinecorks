## Your first sign-in

Each person gets their own account. The Owner (or someone allowed to manage users) adds you in **Settings › Users**, and you receive a one-time setup link by email. Open it, choose a password of at least 10 characters and set up **2-step sign-in** with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password…). Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe.

On the cloud you also enter your **workspace** ID when you sign in; on your own server you do not.

## The screens

- **Overview** — bottles, value and what needs attention (drinking windows, old market prices).
- **Search** — every bottle, with filters; the starting point for most work.
- **Storage** — cellars, lockers and racks, and what is in each.
- **Orders**, **Customers**, **Catalogs** — selling (if your plan includes it).
- **Market prices**, **Reports** — value, cost and profit.
- **Change log** — every change, who made it and what it was before.
- **Settings** — your company, users and roles, wine types, licence and more.

What you see depends on your **role**: if a menu or a figure (such as cost) is missing, your role does not include it.

## Language

Switch between 繁體中文 and English with the button at the top. Each person keeps their own choice; documents for customers use the customer's language.

## Phones

Staff use their own phones in the cellar — no app store needed.

- **Cloud:** open the workspace address in the phone's browser and sign in.
- **Own server:** on the server, open **Start › MyWineCorks › Phone setup** and scan the code with the phone. The first time, the phone asks you to trust the server's certificate; the page explains how for iPhone and Android.

Add the page to the home screen for an app-like icon. The phone keeps a copy of your cellars so scanning works even without signal (see *Moving and counting*).
