## Scan

On the phone, tap **Scan** and point the camera at any label. A bottle shows the wine and its place; a case shows its bottles; a rack shows what should be on it.

## Move

**Move**: scan what you are moving (bottles or a whole case), then scan where it goes (a rack, or a case). Two scans move a case of 12. Moving bottles out of a case or into one is the same.

## Stock-out

**Stock-out**: choose bottles by number, whole cases, or a wine and quantity; choose the reason — own use, gift, tasting, broken, stocktake shortage, entry error or other — and confirm. Sales go through orders instead. A role can have a limit on how many bottles one stock-out may take; above it, ask someone whose role allows more.

## Stocktake

**Stocktakes**: choose a locker and scan every case and loose bottle that is really there (with the phone, or a USB / Bluetooth scanner into the box). **Compare with records** lists cases and bottles found and not found, codes scanned here but recorded elsewhere, and codes not recognised. Fix each one — **Move here**, or **Stock out as shortage** — then **Mark as done**.

## No signal in the cellar

The phone keeps a copy of your cellars. Without a connection you can still scan, move, stock out and count; the phone shows how many changes are waiting and sends them when it is back on the network. If someone changed the same bottle meanwhile, it appears under **Sync conflicts** on the phone and you choose which to keep.
