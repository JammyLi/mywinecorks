## What it can do

- **Label photos** — fill in a new wine from its labels.
- **Invoices** — turn a merchant's invoice into stock-in lines.
- **Search in your words** — turn a sentence into search filters.
- **Drinking windows** — suggest a window for wines that have none.
- **Ask your cellar** — answer questions from your own figures.
- **Price suggestions** — for an order line.
- **Orders from messages** — turn a customer's message or photo into a draft order.
- **Customer portal** — let customers search in their own words and ask about a wine.

## Always a suggestion

AI never changes your data. Every AI result is marked ✦ and must be checked and saved by a person through the normal screen.

## What is sent

Only what the request needs — a label photo, the invoice, the sentence and your list of types and regions — goes through MyWineCorks to the AI service, and is not stored there. Cost and customer prices are only used for people whose role allows them. For **Ask your cellar**, the figures are calculated on your own server; the AI only sees the totals it asked for.

## Switching it on and off

**Settings › AI** has a switch for each helper and shows this month's usage. AI needs the *AI* module in your plan.
