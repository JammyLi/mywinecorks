## Users

**Settings › Users**: add people by email (they get a setup link), choose their role, and switch off anyone who leaves — their history stays. Each user can see and end their own signed-in devices in **Settings › My security**.

## Roles

**Settings › Roles** starts with ready-made roles — Owner, Admin, Manager / Sales, Employee, Picker, Viewer / Accountant — that you can copy and change:

- **What they may do** — per area: view, create, edit, approve…
- **What they may see** — cost, profit, merchants, customer prices, other people's orders.
- **Where** — all cellars, or only some.
- **Limits** — largest order they may confirm, largest price change, largest stock-out.
- **Access** — back office, phone, or both.

Permissions are enforced on the server, so a hidden figure is also missing from exports, PDFs, phones and AI answers.

Only the **Owner** can delete things and clear data.

## Change log

**Change log** records every change: who, when, what it was before and after, and the reason where one is asked. It cannot be edited. Filter it by person, area or item.
