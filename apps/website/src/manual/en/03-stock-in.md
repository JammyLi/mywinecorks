## A new wine

**Search › New wine.** Enter producer, name, vintage (or NV), type, size, and optionally country, region, classification, grapes, drinking window, scores and notes. MyWineCorks warns you if a wine with the same producer, name, vintage and size already exists — use that one instead of creating a duplicate.

With AI switched on, **Fill in from label photo** reads the front and back label for you. Check every field before saving; the wines already in your cellar that look the same are listed.

## Stock-in

On a wine's page choose **Stock in**:

- **How many** bottles, and **where**: loose on a rack, or in a new case (choose its size), or into an existing case.
- **Cost** per bottle (roles that see cost), the **currency** and exchange rate, **extra costs** (shipping, duty), the **merchant**, purchase date and invoice number.

Each stock-in creates bottle numbers; print their labels straight away from the confirmation.

## From a merchant's invoice

**Import › Stock in from invoice** (AI, roles that see cost) reads a PDF or photo of the invoice. Every line is matched to a wine you have — or marked as a new wine — with quantity and unit cost. Choose where the bottles go, check each line, and add them one by one. Nothing is added until you click **Add to stock**.

## Market price and suggested price

The **market price** is what the wine is worth (from Wine-Searcher, an auction or your own judgement), kept with its date so value can be shown over time. Enter it on the wine's page or in bulk in **Market prices** (also from Excel). The **suggested price** is your own selling price; orders and catalogs can use it.
