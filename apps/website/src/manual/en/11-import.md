## From Excel

**Import** offers templates (Chinese and English) for wines and stock, storage and market prices. Upload your file and MyWineCorks:

1. reads the columns (common Chinese and English headings are recognised),
2. lists every problem — missing vintage, unknown rack, a price that is not a number — before anything is saved,
3. suggests merging names that are spelled differently (*Ch. Margaux* / *Château Margaux*, merchants),
4. shows what will be created, and saves only when you confirm.

## From MyWines

Everything moves over — wines, bottles, cases, storage, prices, customers and orders — and the bottle, case and wine numbers stay the same, so printed labels keep working. We do the move with you.

## Taking data out

- Any search, list or report → **Excel**.
- **Settings › License › Export my data** (Owner) → all wines to Excel, or download the whole database.

Even if a licence has expired, the Owner can still sign in and export. Your data is never deleted by the software.
