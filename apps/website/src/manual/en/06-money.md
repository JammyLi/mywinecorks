## Where the numbers come from

- **Cost** — what each bottle cost when it came in (plus its share of extra costs), converted to your currency at the purchase-day rate.
- **Market value** — the latest market price of each wine × bottles in stock.
- **Sales** — order lines of shipped orders, after discounts, without tax.

## Reports

**Reports** shows:

- **Collection value** — bottles, market value, purchase cost and unrealised gain, the value over the last 12 months, broken down by type, country, region, cellar, locker or wine; storage rent against value when a rent is set on a cellar.
- **Sales and profit** — orders, revenue (shipped, excluding tax) and profit for a period, by month, customer or wine.

Purchases by merchant, region or month can be answered by **Ask your cellar**. Only roles allowed to see cost and profit see those columns — in reports, exports, PDFs and AI answers alike.

## Asking in your own words (AI)

**Ask your cellar** answers questions such as *“How much did we spend on Burgundy this year?”* or *“Which wines have doubled in value?”*. The figures are calculated on your own server from your data, with your permissions, and wines in the answer link to their pages.
